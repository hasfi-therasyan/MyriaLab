Services

What we actually build.

Four recurring shapes of work. Most engagements touch two or three of these at once, not just one.

AI-Native Development

AI features that earn their place in the codebase.

We integrate models where they measurably help, test them like any other code path, and remove them the moment a simpler heuristic does the job as well.

  • Model integration inside existing product code, not a bolted-on sidecar
  • Evaluation harnesses so a model change is a tested diff, not a vibe
  • Fallback paths for when the model is wrong, slow, or unavailable
Platform & Infrastructure

Foundations built to bend, not break.

Systems designed for the scale clients expect next year, not just the load they're carrying today.

  • Deployment pipelines that make shipping boring, in the good way
  • Observability wired in from the first service, not bolted on after an incident
  • Cost and capacity planning that's revisited, not written once and forgotten
Product Engineering

Interfaces people actually choose to open.

We judge software the way users do: by whether it respects their time, not by how it looks in a pitch deck.

  • End-to-end ownership from the first wireframe to the production rollout
  • Design systems that keep the product consistent as more people build on it
  • Performance budgets treated as a feature requirement, not an afterthought
Developer Experience

Tooling that makes the next engineer's day easier.

Internal libraries, CI pipelines, and documentation get the same care as anything we ship externally, because slow builds and unclear code cost real engineering hours.

  • Build and test pipelines fast enough that nobody avoids running them
  • Internal documentation written for the engineer joining in six months
  • Shared libraries that get maintained, not abandoned after the first release
